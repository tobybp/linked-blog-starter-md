## Summary Questions
1) Name the main components of a CAT scanner:
	X-ray tube, detector, motorised sliding table
2) State one advantage and one disadvantage of a CAT scan over an X-ray image
	An advantage is that it provides depth information in the form of a 3d scan compared to the x-ray only giving a 2d result, however a disadvantage is that it greatly increases the exposure of the patient to x-rays, increasing risk of mutation of cells.
3) Suggest why a thin beam of X-rays is necessary in a CAT scanner.
	To create a thin slice for an image, for a precise location for any defects in the human body.
